## 🧠 About this system

Operations professional by experience, curious human by default, aspiring builder by questionable life choices.

I've spent years figuring out how businesses work, why processes break, and how people manage to make simple things unnecessarily complicated.

Now I'm exploring data, software, and AI to see what happens when I stop just figuring out how things work and start building things myself.

I ask questions. I investigate. I connect dots that probably weren't supposed to be connected.

Sometimes this leads to useful projects. Sometimes it leads to three hours of research about something completely unrelated to the original question.

Both outcomes are considered learning.
